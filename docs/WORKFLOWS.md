# User Workflows

This document describes the app's current user-facing workflows. See
[features.md](features.md) for detailed behavior and known limitations.

## Creating a Bill

1. Tap the add floating action button.
2. Enter a required name.
3. Optionally enter a value.
4. Select a due date.
5. Choose the bill's notification and monthly recurrence settings.
6. Tap **Save Bill**.

New bills are pending. The create form defaults notification and recurrence to
off, independently of the global notification preferences.

## Managing Bills

1. Select the Pending or Paid tab.
2. Tap a bill to open its edit screen.
3. Edit fields and tap **Save Bill**, or use **Pay Bill**/**Unpay Bill**.
4. Use **Delete Bill** to remove the bill.

There is no payment-status checkbox in the home list.

## Setting Up Notifications

1. Start the app and respond to notification permission prompts.
2. Open **Notification Settings** from the home menu.
3. Change the startup, per-bill, or daily preference.

The current scheduling effects and limitations are defined in
[Notification Settings](features.md#notification-settings).

## Calendar Navigation

1. Tap the calendar floating action button.
2. Navigate with arrows, a horizontal swipe, or the month/year picker.
3. Tap a date to view its bills.
4. Tap a bill to open its edit screen.
